**Learning TkCurses**, and with it tkinter, is four pages read in order:
the [introduction](introduction.md), for someone new to both;
[core concepts](concepts.md) - the widget tree, geometry management and
events; [common widgets](widgets.md); and
[a larger program](larger-program.md), built a piece at a time.
[Common mistakes](mistakes.md) are the ones nearly everybody makes once.

[Porting a tkinter program](porting.md) is for someone bringing one over.
[Terminals](terminals.md) is about where a program runs: what a terminal can
show, which keys it can send, the mouse, the clipboard, and the settings
that adjust TkCurses to a terminal. [Examples](examples.md) are twelve
complete programs, each with the screen it draws.

[How TkCurses works](how-it-works.md) follows a key press and a redraw
through the library's layers, and the [glossary](glossary.md) defines the
words all of these use.
